CONFIRMED = seen in fetched source text (abs page / HTML / locally extracted PDF text). UNCONFIRMED = search snippet or memory.
Our convention: omega=(T-t)^-1 Omega(x/(T-t)^(1+lambda)); CLM lambda=0.

## 2. Independent classical values for a=-1
- Lushnikov-Silantyev-Siegel arXiv:2010.01201 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.01201, v2 23 Aug 2021, J Nonlinear Sci 31:82; HTML https://arxiv.org/html/2010.01201, read to char 200000 of ~259k). Convention: omega=f(xi)/tau, xi=x/tau^alpha, tau=t_c-t, so alpha=1+lambda (alpha=1 at a=0). Table 1 at a=-1: alpha(time fit)=2.208, alpha_2(spatial fit)=2.206; no eigenvalue alpha_e at a<=-0.5. Versus 1+1.18078=2.18078 these fits are about 1.2% off (2-3 digits only). Eigenvalue digits are given only for -0.25..0 (a=-0.25: 1.296593455; a=-0.1: 1.121312899). "CCF" is not named; no higher or unstable branches. a_c=0.6890665337007457. CONFIRMED.
- Eggers-Fontelos Nonlinearity 33:325 (PDF https://people.maths.bris.ac.uk/~majge/Eggers_2020_Nonlinearity_33_325.pdf, fetched): their Burgers-type fractional equation at the Hilbert limit has "scaling exponent 1.181..."; they find an infinite sequence of unstable branches expected, explored the first unstable branch only, shown in a figure (no digits extracted). CONFIRMED (the identification with our lambda is my inference).
- Huang-Qin-Wang-Wei arXiv:2305.05895 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.05895; ARMA doi 10.1007/s00205-024-01971-3): abstract only; no exponent digits.
- Huang-Tong-Wang arXiv:2603.25104 (https://arxiv.org/html/2603.25104, v4 23 Oct 2026; read to char 200000 of ~238k). Their a<0 scenario is a different two-scale blowup with a singular outer profile from degenerate data. Table 5.1 at a=-1: c_l~1.4541, c_omega~-0.8634, gamma~1.6841; explicit singular family has gamma=1-a=2. Not the smooth CCF lambda=1.18 profile. It says NN work found one stable plus multiple unstable CCF profiles and the stable one matches a fixed-point profile from its ref [26]; uniqueness not established. Convergence for a<0 is numerical only. (Fetch-tool paraphrase.)
- No classical non-NN paper found with more than ~3 digits of lambda at a=-1 other than the ODIL paper (item 4).

- NO paper found (2023 to Nov 2026) proving existence of an UNSTABLE self-similar profile for CCF, gCLM, Burgers-type, Boussinesq or Euler. 2509.14185 and 2511.22819 say only that accuracy is sufficient for a CAP; 2509.14185 says a proof is in preparation. Gomez-Serrano March 2026 slides (https://www.nas.nasa.gov/assets/nas/pdf/ams/2026/AMS_20260305_Gome-Serrano.pdf) were unreadable (compressed); search snippet only: ML profile + CAP, topological argument for unstable manifold. UNCONFIRMED.
- Related CAP work (not unstable): Chen-Hou Boussinesq/Euler with boundary (arXiv:2210.07191, 2305.05660), titles only. 2026 titles seen only in search and not opened: arXiv:2609.10867 (Euler on R^3, PINN + spline certification), 2608.15174, 2605.15149. UNCONFIRMED.
- Stable CCF / a<0 rigorous:
  * Huang-Qin-Wang-Wei 2305.05895: exact self-similar blowup with interiorly smooth profiles for ALL a<=1 via a fixed-point map (CONFIRMED abstract). I did not see that it identifies lambda=1.1808 at a=-1; no CAP; stability not proven. That the NN stable profile matches it is claimed by 2603.25104 (identity of [26] UNCONFIRMED).
  * Chen-Hou-Huang arXiv:1905.06387 (https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.06387, CPAM 74 (2021) 1282): asymptotically self-similar blowup for De Gregorio and generalized De Gregorio over the "entire range of parameter" for Holder-continuous compactly supported data, via energy method on approximate profile. Abstract only. Does not give the smooth CCF lambda.
  * Zheng arXiv:2209.09886 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.09886): exactly self-similar C^alpha blowup for alpha in (0,1) with |a*alpha| small. Journal ref (Nonlinearity 36 (2023) 5252) UNCONFIRMED. Whether a=-1 is covered is not established (small |a*alpha| required).
  * Castro-Cordoba: blowup for a<0 by Lyapunov argument (cited in 2010.01201 text); no profile.
  * Open per these sources: rigorous existence/stability of the smooth stable lambda=1.1808 profile with its exponent, and all unstable ones.

## 5. Far field and analyticity
- 2610.30492 Supplement S3 (CONFIRMED): dominant balance gives sigma_q=-c/(1+lambda). De Gregorio family: Omega ~ |y|^(-1/(1+lambda)), U ~ |y|^(lambda/(1+lambda)), mapping exponent alpha=1/(1+lambda) (ccf0 0.4586, ccf1 0.6228, ccf2 0.6797). The normalized profile Omega*|y|^(1/(1+lambda)) satisfies an ODE in s=y^(-1/(1+lambda)) with analytic right-hand side and free initial value, hence analytic in s (power expansion in y^(-1/(1+lambda))); non-integer branch exponent gives only algebraic Chebyshev decay. A derivation by the authors, not a theorem; tail amplitude is free (fixed by normalization).
- 2509.14185 Methods: tail "dominated by some of the linear terms"; power-law decay enforced by an envelope; for Boussinesq |y|^(-1/(1+lambda)); for CCF no lambda-vs-order law found. CONFIRMED.
- 2603.25104 tail |x|^(-1/(1+|a|)) is for the different singular family.
- 2511.22819 selects lambda via the sign of the relative residual at the origin (non-smooth origin signal). CONFIRMED paraphrase.
